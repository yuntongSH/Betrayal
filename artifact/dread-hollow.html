🕯️ Dread Hollow

A manor that builds itself, one dreadful room at a time — until one of you stops being a friend. Pick your party (pass-and-play on one screen) and begin the descent.

This is an offline single-device build. The full online multiplayer version lives at the GitHub repo.

Drag to orbit · scroll to zoom · click a glowing room to move · click a flame arrow to explore · click a monster to strike
