🕯️ Dread Hollow

A manor that builds itself, one dreadful room at a time — until one of you stops being a friend. Pick your party (pass-and-play on one screen) and begin the descent.

Difficulty

Pass-and-play on one screen, or play solo against bots. Small parties are topped up to 3 with bots. The full online multiplayer version lives in the GitHub repo.

Drag to orbit · arrow keys / WASD to move · click a glowing room or flame arrow · click a monster to strike

How to play

Dread Hollow is a haunted-house betrayal game in two acts. You and your party explore a manor that builds itself — until the house turns and one of you (or the house itself) becomes the enemy.

Act I — Explore

  • On your turn you have Movement equal to your Speed. Click a glowing room to walk there, or a flame arrow to push through a door into a new, unknown room.
  • Wandering back the way you came is free — only your net distance from where the turn began costs movement.
  • New rooms reveal cards: Items to carry, Events that befall you, and Omens. Each Omen risks a roll that can trigger the haunt.
  • Deliberate actions cost one step each: 🔍 Search a room for loot, 👁 Investigate to read what's coming, ✚ Steady yourself to heal, ⛓ Barricade a door shut.

Your four traits

  • Speed, Might, Sanity, Knowledge. Each sits on a track; damage slides it down. The leftmost space is a skull — reach it and you die.
  • Rolls use dice numbered 0, 1, 2. You roll a number of them equal to the trait being tested; higher totals win.

Act II — The Haunt

  • When the house turns, a scenario begins: a traitor is revealed (or the house itself rises). You'll see only your own goal — the other side's is secret.
  • Now you can attack: click a monster (or foe) sharing your room. Some horrors strike at the body (Might), others at the mind (Sanity ✦).
  • Reach your goal first to win. Every haunt is different.
